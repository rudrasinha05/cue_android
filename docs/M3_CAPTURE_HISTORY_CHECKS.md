# Cue capture and history device checks

The CI gate assembles a debug APK and runs the compressed-history round-trip and corruption tests. It does not replace these phone checks. Run on `develop` without clearing app data so Room v2 → v4 migration is exercised.

1. Open an existing install. Confirm its reminders and alerts still exist, then create, edit, snooze, complete and archive reminders. Inbox should show each change, fired alert, title, time and original source. Tap a document source and confirm it opens on the original device; on another device its saved excerpt should still be visible.
2. From another app, **Share** text to Cue; select text and use **Cue** in the selection menu. Each should open the same compact review sheet. Cancel without saving, then retry and save. Source text should be visible in Inbox after saving.
3. In AI, use **Talk to Cue** and inspect the transcription before saving. Say “tomorrow at 9 pm” and confirm the suggested alert can be edited; ambiguous text must remain for manual review. Test a phone without a speech recognizer or with no network: the app should show a useful message and manual capture should still work.
4. Import a TXT, CSV/TSV, Markdown and DOCX document, plus a clear photo and a PDF of up to 20 pages. OCR currently reads Latin script. The review shows at most 4,000 characters; the source history keeps a 2,000-character excerpt and a link to the local original. Inspect the extraction, cancel one import, and save another. Revoking document permission should not erase saved history.
5. In airplane mode, save/edit reminders, then reconnect and sign in. On another signed-in device, verify the reminder, source and event timeline. Sign out and confirm another account cannot see them.
6. With at least 20 synced events older than 30 days, trigger a sync. Confirm Inbox still shows every event in order; kill/restart Cue and repeat. The GZIP payload must pass event count and SHA-256 checks. Cloud originals are retained until server-side compaction is implemented.
7. On **You**, try to enable **Floating Cue** while signed out: the switch must stay unavailable. Sign in, enable it, and grant Android's **Display over other apps** permission. The small bubble should appear above another app. Drag it around, release near each edge, and confirm it snaps partly behind that edge. Tap it and use Voice Reminder, Quick Reminder, Import / Scan, Ask AI, My Day and Assistant Settings. The first three must reach the existing review/import flow; My Day filters today's reminders; Ask AI currently opens the capture hub. Adjust collapsed opacity in You and check the bubble changes while expanded actions remain readable. Rotate the phone and repeat. Turn the switch off, revoke overlay permission in Android settings, and sign out; each must remove the bubble without removing reminders. Re-enable only from Cue.
8. In **You**, enable **Notification shortcuts** and grant notification access if prompted. Tap the Cue notification to open the six-action sheet; test Voice and Quick direct buttons. When Floating Cue is also on, its required foreground notification carries those buttons and the separate panel notification disappears. Disable the panel while keeping the bubble on, then disable the bubble while keeping the panel on. Revoke notification access or the Cue controls channel: shortcuts must stop without deleting reminders; enabling permission again requires an explicit switch-on in Cue. Verify that reminder alerts still use their separate notification channel.
