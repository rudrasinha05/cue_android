# Cue capture and history device checks

The CI gate assembles a debug APK and runs the compressed-history round-trip and corruption tests. It does not replace these phone checks. Run on `develop` without clearing app data so Room v2 → v4 migration is exercised.

1. Open an existing install. Confirm its reminders and alerts still exist, then create, edit, snooze, complete and archive reminders. Inbox should show each change, fired alert, title, time and original source. Tap a document source and confirm it opens on the original device; on another device its saved excerpt should still be visible.
2. From another app, **Share** text to Cue; select text and use **Cue** in the selection menu. A single clear future action should save in the background and notify the result. Ambiguous content should offer Review from the notification, opening the editor once without reprocessing. Source text should be visible in Inbox after saving.
3. On Reminders, tap **Speak**. Say “Call Mom tomorrow at 9 pm” and verify automatic save and notification; vague speech must offer review. Try “Kal subah 9:30 baje meeting yaad dilana” and an ambiguous “Kal 5 baje” to verify the time confidence boundary. Test a phone without a speech recognizer or with no network: the app should show a useful message and manual capture should still work.
4. From Reminders, import a TXT, CSV/TSV, Markdown and DOCX document, plus a clear photo and a PDF of up to 20 pages. OCR currently reads Latin script. A single clear action should auto-save and notify, while multiple or vague deadlines should offer review. The source history keeps a short excerpt and a link to the local original. Revoking document permission should not erase saved history.
5. In airplane mode, save/edit reminders, then reconnect and sign in. On another signed-in device, verify the reminder, source and event timeline. Sign out and confirm another account cannot see them.
6. With at least 20 synced events older than 30 days, trigger a sync. Confirm Inbox lists older archive cards by date and count without opening them. Tap a card and confirm its events appear in order; kill/restart Cue and repeat. Search a title present in a new indexed archive, then open it and confirm the matching event. Older archives migrated with an empty index must remain manually browseable. The GZIP payload must pass event count and SHA-256 checks. For new verified batches, the cloud prunes only the server-recorded event IDs after upload; older batches without IDs retain raw recovery copies. Verify a second device restores exact count/checksum after pruning.
7. On **You**, try to enable **Floating Cue** while signed out: the switch must stay unavailable. Sign in, enable it, and grant Android's **Display over other apps** permission. The small bubble should appear above another app. Drag it around, release near each edge, and confirm it snaps partly behind that edge. Tap it and use Voice Reminder, Quick Reminder, Import / Scan, My Day and Assistant Settings. Drop text and supported documents on the collapsed bubble; verify one result notification, no duplicate when repeated, and the Share → Cue fallback if the source app withholds file access. Adjust collapsed opacity and size in You, rotate, then turn the switch off, revoke overlay permission, and sign out; each must remove the bubble without removing reminders.
8. In **You**, enable **Notification shortcuts** and grant notification access if prompted. Tap the Cue notification to open the five-action sheet; test Voice and Quick direct buttons. When Floating Cue is also on, its required foreground notification carries those buttons and the separate panel notification disappears. Disable the panel while keeping the bubble on, then disable the bubble while keeping the panel on. Revoke notification access or the Cue controls channel: shortcuts must stop without deleting reminders; enabling permission again requires an explicit switch-on in Cue. Verify that reminder alerts still use their separate notification channel.

9. On **You**, choose each color theme in Light and Dark, then visit Reminders, Upcoming, Inbox and You. Inspect backgrounds, cards, text, menus, sheets and the collapsed/expanded bubble. Restart the app and confirm the choice persists. The Android notification panel follows system styling.
10. Drag selected text from an app that supports global drag onto the collapsed Cue bubble. It should acknowledge without opening the bubble or Cue: a single future actionable line saves once; a second drop reports it already exists; uncertain input posts a Review notification. Tap Review and edit before saving. Drop an image and a PDF/DOCX URI from a compatible source, then revoke source access and confirm an understandable failure notification with Share → Cue fallback. Some apps do not grant cross-app URI access to overlays, so also test Share → Cue for these items. Turn the capture notification channel off and verify the app does not crash.
11. In **You**, enable **Morning plan notification**, set waking/bed hours, and tap **View today's schedule**. Fixed reminders must stay at their exact time; an overlapping meal must not hide them. Untimed active items get free slots once, while completed items do not appear. Try a reminder outside waking hours. Change timezone/reboot and check the next morning's summary notification and its My Day shortcut. Disable the switch and confirm the morning alarm stops. Revoke notifications and check the switch does not falsely promise a visible alert.
12. On Android 14+, enable the **Screen analysis** switch in You. Choose **Entire screen** or one app in Android's system picker, allow the visible foreground notification, and display one clear actionable future deadline in a busy screen. Verify that it creates one item and acknowledges in the notification panel. Multiple candidate deadlines, disconnected text, past deadlines, and ordinary content must add nothing; repeated frames must not duplicate reminders. Inspect history: a full screen dump or frame must not be saved. Lock the device and verify sampling pauses. Stop from both Cue and the notification. Repeat after permission cancellation, rotation, sign-out, and backgrounding. Android asks for projection consent each session; this is not a persistent grant. On Android 13 and earlier the switch stays unavailable.
13. In You, turn on **Find deadlines in notifications**, approve Cue's notification access in Android settings, and return to Cue. Post a test notification from another app containing one line such as “Submit report tomorrow 5:30 pm”; verify one reminder, a notification acknowledgement, and a notification source in Inbox. Repost it, then post a multi-deadline or vague message and verify no duplicate or false reminder. Turn the switch off and post another clear deadline; verify no new reminder. Revoke access in Android settings and verify the switch turns off. Cue's own notifications must never be ingested. Check that no full notification body is retained.
14. In You, export guest data to JSON. Verify every reminder, source and event is present. With a signed-in account and at least one compressed history batch, export again online: verify each archived event appears exactly once and all source IDs survive. Cancel the document picker and verify nothing changes. Corrupt a test batch checksum and verify export fails instead of silently omitting events. For a guest profile, cancel the delete confirmation once, then confirm it and verify reminders, history, archives and pending alarms are gone after restart. For a signed-in profile, export first; test cancel and offline failure, then confirm **Delete my Cue data** and check that cloud/device data do not return. Separately, test **Delete Cue account**: cancel, retry offline, then delete account A and confirm its Auth profile and Cue data are gone while account B stays intact. Google identity is not deleted.
15. Sign into two devices with one reminder. Sync both, then edit different titles and alert times offline on each. Sync device A, then device B. Verify B receives A's current version and no stale B alarm fires; B's losing draft appears under **Earlier device edit saved** in Inbox with its original details/time. Reopen the app, sync again and ensure the conflict event is not duplicated. Repeat with simultaneous updates while online; a version race should remain pending for retry instead of overwriting the other device. Verify a guest migration retry leaves an already edited cloud reminder unchanged.

Pending capture work: device QA for overlay drag/URI grants, channel permissions, screen projection lifecycle, model download and offline/revoked access; provider-backed Ask AI responses are later intelligence work. Play review of the user-enabled overlay and media projection foreground services is part of P5. Pending history work: two-device verification of cloud archive pruning, older batch backfill, semantic source deduplication and import metadata/permissions.

12. Capture the same future reminder once by voice/share and again through a different source with case or punctuation differences. Confirm only one active alarm and reminder exists, the second capture acknowledges its linked source, and Inbox shows the new source and source-added event. Repeat an identical drop and confirm its source is not duplicated. Use a different due time or action and confirm it stays a separate reminder.

13. In You → Floating Cue, move the Bubble size slider below opacity through its range. Confirm the collapsed drag target resizes and can still accept a text drop at the smallest size, the expanded menu remains usable, the bubble stays docked to its chosen edge, and the selected size survives app restart and service restoration.

14. With many archived batches, open Inbox and inspect that only batch metadata appears initially. Tap one archive, verify only that batch decompresses and its event count/checksum error is surfaced rather than silently omitted. Close it and verify its events leave the list. Room v5→v6 migration must retain existing batch payloads.
